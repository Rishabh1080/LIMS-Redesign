## Design Context

### Users

LIMS V3 is used by laboratory operations teams in QA/QC and analytical environments, including lab managers, technicians, quality analysts, supervisors, and administrators. They use the product during focused operational work to configure testing rules, manage samples and test requests, enter results, review approvals, and maintain laboratory records.

### Brand Personality

Precise, dependable, and restrained. The interface should build confidence through clear hierarchy, consistent controls, compact information density, and familiar operational patterns rather than decorative flourish.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing light-mode Sampleify LIMS design system and shared application shell as the source of truth. Screens should feel calm, clinical, and workmanlike: pale neutral page backgrounds, white work surfaces, crisp borders, modest radii, blue interaction accents, and compact typography. Attached product screenshots and source pages are replication references, not prompts for redesign.

### Design Principles

- Preserve the shared shell and established `smplfy` component language across every page.
- Prioritize exact field order, labels, values, spacing, and states for design-to-code handoff work.
- Keep operational forms readable through a narrow centered measure and consistent vertical rhythm.
- Use progressive, familiar interactions with strong keyboard focus and clear required-field indicators.
- Avoid inventing data or behavior that is not present in the supplied design or source.
